Kartomed dictionaries

The reference dictionaries the Kartomed app (foleks.com/kartomed) downloads to keep them current between its releases. The app only downloads these files, without an account and without sending anything about its user.

Kartomed is an unofficial app: it does not represent any government body and is not affiliated with one.

Sources

  • Diagnoses, Ukraine — national classification of diseases NK 025:2021, State Expert Center of the Ministry of Health of Ukraine (dec.gov.ua)
  • Diagnoses, Russia — ICD-10 classification of diseases, reference data of the Russian Ministry of Health, OID 1.2.643.5.1.13.13.11.1005 (nsi.rosminzdrav.ru)
  • Diagnoses, USA — English names of diagnoses, ICD-10-CM classification (cdc.gov)
  • Medicines — State Register of Medicinal Products of Ukraine, open data (data.gov.ua)
  • Symptoms, doctor specialties, vaccines and allergens — the developer's own lists.
